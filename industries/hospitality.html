Hospitality & F&B

Hotels, resorts, restaurants and guest-facing operations.

What we keep finding in this sector

  • Guest messages scattered across WhatsApp, OTAs, email, Google and Instagram
  • No single view of bookings, reviews and revenue across channels
  • Review requests that depend on a staff member remembering
  • Owners reconciling channel revenue by hand every month

Hospitality has a channel problem, not a software problem

A mid-sized Indian hotel typically runs a PMS, an OTA channel manager, a Google Business profile, an accounting package, an Instagram account and at least two WhatsApp numbers.

Every one of those works. None of them talk to each other. So a complaint that arrives on WhatsApp at 11pm never reaches the guest's PMS record, the front desk apologises for something already resolved, and the owner spends the first week of every month reconciling revenue by hand.

The unified guest inbox

The single highest-return build in this sector is bringing every guest conversation into one workflow — WhatsApp, OTA messaging, email, Google and social — where each message carries the guest's booking history alongside it.

Staff stop switching between six applications. Nothing gets missed because it arrived on the wrong channel.

Follow-ups that actually happen

Review requests, feedback collection and post-stay follow-up are all things everyone agrees are important and nobody does consistently, because they depend on a busy person remembering at the right moment.

Automated, timed properly and written to sound like a human, these move review volume measurably. Reviews move OTA ranking. OTA ranking moves bookings.

The owner's daily view

Occupancy, ADR, channel mix, review sentiment, open guest issues and the things that need a decision today — delivered to WhatsApp each morning rather than sitting behind a login.
