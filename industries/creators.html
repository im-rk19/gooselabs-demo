Creators & Agencies

Content teams, influencers, talent agencies and production studios.

What we keep finding in this sector

  • Brand deals tracked in DMs and screenshots
  • Deliverables and deadlines living in one person's head
  • Shoot, travel and publishing calendars that constantly collide
  • No consolidated view of performance across platforms

The content is the easy part

Creators who reach a certain scale hit the same wall, and it is never creative. It is operational.

Brand conversations happen across Instagram DMs, email and WhatsApp. Deliverables are agreed verbally. Deadlines exist in one person's memory. Shoot schedules collide with travel. Invoices go out late because nobody is quite sure what was delivered.

Where the money leaks

Missed deliverables damage relationships with brands, which is the expensive kind of damage. Late invoicing is the visible symptom of an unclear record of what was actually agreed.

Both come from the same root cause: no single place where the deal, the deliverables and the dates live together.

What we build

A brand and agency CRM that tracks conversations from first contact through delivery and payment. A calendar that holds shoots, publishing dates, travel and deliverable deadlines in one view. Contract and deliverable tracking against what was agreed. Performance data pulled from connected platforms.

Small teams, real constraints

Most creator operations are two or three people. Anything requiring a dedicated administrator will not be used. We build for the reality that the person managing operations is also the person in front of the camera.
