Product & MVP Builds

The smallest version of your product that real users will actually use.

What changes

  • A working product in front of real users inside a quarter
  • Usage data that tells you what to build next
  • A codebase your own team can take over

What we deliver

  • Product definition - users, screens, data model, business logic
  • Mobile or web application build
  • Admin panel and internal tooling
  • Analytics instrumentation from day one
  • Handover documentation and code ownership

Building everything is how products die

The most common failure we see is not bad engineering. It is scope. A twelve-month build, a big launch, and the discovery that the feature everyone argued about is the one nobody uses.

What we do instead

We find the single workflow that proves the product is worth using, build that properly, and put it in front of real users fast.

WhatsOn is a fair example. It could have been a full social platform. It launched as events, RSVPs and QR check-ins for one community — the three things that actually mattered to Yi Raipur's members. Everything since has been added because usage data asked for it.

What we build

Mobile applications for consumers and field teams. Web platforms and customer portals. Admin panels and internal tools — genuinely the highest-return category, because the people using them are the people you pay.

Analytics are not optional

Every build ships instrumented. Not because dashboards are nice, but because the second version should be decided by what people did, not by whoever is most senior in the room.

Handover

We would rather your team ran it eventually. The code, the documentation and the deployment pipeline are yours, and we will train whoever inherits them.

Common questions

Do we own the code?

Yes, entirely. We hand over the repository, the documentation and the deployment setup. Vendor lock-in is a bad way to keep a client.

How small is an MVP?

Smaller than you want it to be. The goal is not a reduced version of the full product - it is the one workflow that proves people will change their behaviour for it.

Can you take over an existing half-built product?

Often yes. We will audit it first and tell you honestly whether continuing or restarting is cheaper. Both answers happen.

Thinking about Product & MVP Builds?

Tell us what the problem looks like day to day. If we do not think this is the right starting point for you, we will say so.
