Healthcare AI Systems

Clinical documentation, patient journeys and outcome tracking built for Indian clinics.

What changes

  • Consultation notes written while the doctor talks, not after clinic hours
  • One patient record instead of paper files, WhatsApp photos and spreadsheets

Healthcare is where we have the most evidence

Most AI vendors selling into Indian healthcare have never run a clinic. We build alongside one.

GooseLabs' healthcare work started inside Prānaa, a regenerative medicine and longevity clinic in Shankar Nagar, Raipur, where our founder is technology lead. Everything on this page has been through the unglamorous test of real doctors, real patients and real front-desk staff who will abandon any system that slows them down.

The problem we keep finding

Indian clinics rarely lack data. They lack a single place to put it.

Intake happens on paper. Diagnostics arrive as PDFs over WhatsApp. Consultation notes live in the doctor's own shorthand. Follow-up depends on whether someone at the desk remembers. By the time leadership asks how a treatment programme is performing, reconstructing the answer takes a week of manual work.

What we build

We start with the highest-friction step in your specific clinic, not with a platform diagram.

For a diagnostics-heavy practice that is usually structured intake and reporting. For a consultation-heavy practice it is almost always documentation — an AI assistant that listens to the doctor-patient conversation and produces a structured, EMR-ready note. For a programme-based practice it is adherence and outcome tracking across a three-month journey.

Each module works on its own and connects to the others when you are ready.

How we work

A four-week discovery on your floor, watching how the clinic actually runs. A prioritised build sequence with the highest-friction step first. Then fortnightly releases your team can use immediately.

We do not disappear for six months and return with a platform nobody asked for.

Common questions

Do you replace our existing hospital software?

Usually not. Most clinics already have a billing or lab system that works. We build the intelligence layer around it and integrate, rather than asking a working practice to migrate everything at once.

Where does patient data sit?

On infrastructure you control, in India. We design for data residency from the start because healthcare data should not leave the organisation that is accountable for it.

How long before a clinic sees anything usable?

A first working module typically lands in four to six weeks. We build the piece that removes the most manual work first, so the team feels the benefit before the full platform exists.
