Data Intelligence & Dashboards

One number everyone agrees on, instead of four spreadsheets that disagree.

What changes

  • A single source of truth leadership actually trusts
  • Weekly reporting that assembles itself
  • Decisions made from current numbers rather than last month's

What we deliver

  • Data pipeline design and consolidation
  • Leadership and operational dashboards
  • Automated weekly and monthly reporting
  • Alerting on the metrics that matter
  • Definition documentation so metrics stop being argued about

The problem is rarely the chart

It is that three departments define "active customer" differently, so every leadership meeting starts by arguing about whose number is right.

We start with definitions, not visuals

Before building anything we agree what each metric means, where it comes from and who owns it. This is the unglamorous part and it is why most dashboard projects fail — a beautiful dashboard built on contested definitions gets ignored within a month.

Then we remove the manual assembly

In most businesses we work with, someone spends a meaningful part of every week copying figures between spreadsheets to produce a report. That person is usually capable of much more valuable work.

Once the pipeline is built, the report assembles itself and arrives where leadership already looks — often WhatsApp rather than a login nobody remembers.

Operational, not just financial

Financial dashboards tell you about a closed period. The dashboards that change behaviour are operational: which patients missed follow-ups this week, which rooms are underperforming, where production stalled yesterday.

Alerting matters more than reporting

A dashboard requires someone to remember to look. An alert finds them. We usually build both, and the alerting earns its keep faster.

Common questions

Our data is messy. Is that a problem?

It is the normal starting condition and part of the work. What matters is whether the data exists somewhere - cleaning is tractable, inventing is not.

Which tools do you use?

Whatever fits the organisation. Sometimes a proper BI tool, sometimes a well-built web dashboard, sometimes a scheduled WhatsApp report. The best dashboard is the one people open.

How is this different from what our accountant produces?

Financial reporting tells you what happened. Operational dashboards tell you what is happening now, while you can still change it.
